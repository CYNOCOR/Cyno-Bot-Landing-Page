How we handle your details

Last updated 21 November 2026

Who we are

This site is run by CYNOCOR, a technology team based in Colombo, Sri Lanka, which is building CYNO BOT. You can reach us at cynocorofficial@gmail.com or +94 74 232 1760.

What we collect on this website

We only collect what you type into the demo request form:

  • your name and email address (required)
  • your organisation and message, if you choose to add them
  • whether you asked for a demo or to join the waitlist

This site sets no cookies, runs no advertising trackers and does not ask for your location.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your request: to arrange a demo, or to tell you when CYNO BOT is available. We use it because you agreed to it when you sent the form. We never sell it or share it for marketing.

Who else handles it

The form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (opens in a new tab), and kept in our Gmail inbox. Both services may store it outside Sri Lanka.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after our last conversation with you, then we delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, you can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email cynocorofficial@gmail.com and we’ll respond as soon as we can, and within the time the Act allows.

About the robot itself

CYNO BOT uses its camera only to notice that someone is nearby. Images are processed on the robot and not stored. Face recognition is switched off unless a visitor clearly agrees, a visible notice shows when the camera is in use, and a privacy mode turns it off. The robot always says it is a robot.
